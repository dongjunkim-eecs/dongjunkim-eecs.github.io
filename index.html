Research Goal
AI has achieved remarkable performance, yet most models still rely on powerful cloud GPUs. I believe AI should be able to operate efficiently and adaptively across diverse computing platforms, from cloud servers to resource-constrained edge devices.
My research goal is to bridge advanced AI algorithms and efficient computing systems through Algorithm-Hardware Co-Design, enabling AI models that are both deployable and adaptable under real-world hardware constraints.

On-ieum
Integrated AIoT System for the Digitally Vulnerable · Raspberry Pi 5 + Jetson Orin Nano
Public transit guidance kiosk designed for elderly users. Built on Raspberry Pi 5 + Hailo AI HAT+ for face recognition (ArcFace) and Faster-Whisper STT, with gRPC communication to Jetson Orin Nano running an EXAONE LLM. Integrates Kakao/ODsay transit APIs and USB thermal receipt printing. Full-stack development from Flask UI to edge inference pipeline.
